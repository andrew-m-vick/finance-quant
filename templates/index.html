Accounting work

Each tool runs on real accounting logic with a fictional sample company loaded. Click one and press the button; it takes about ten seconds to see.

Trial Balance → Financial Statements

Accounting cycleGAAP presentationClosing entries

Paste an adjusted trial balance; get a multi-step income statement, retained earnings statement, classified balance sheet, closing entries, post-closing TB, and ratios.

If the TB doesn't balance, it tells you why: transpositions (divisible by 9), wrong-side postings, omitted accounts.

Bank Reconciliation

Month-end closeCash controlsAdjusting entries

Matches the cash ledger to the bank statement by check number, amount, and date; classifies deposits in transit, outstanding checks, NSF items, and fees.

Catches the recording error in the sample (a $429 check booked as $492) and drafts the correcting journal entry.

How these are built

  • Money math in exact decimal, never floating point, so ledgers tie to the cent.
  • Automated tests check that the debits equal the credits, that A = L + E, and that the reconciliation ties.
  • Every rule is written up, with sources, on the methodology page.
  • Your data stays private: nothing is stored, and there's no database.
